virtiofsd, as distributed with HiveWorker

This is an unmodified build of virtiofsd, the vhost-user virtio-fs device
backend, from https://gitlab.com/virtio-fs/virtiofsd. HiveWorker's installer
downloads it only on systems that do not package a virtiofsd that runs
without root.

virtiofsd is licensed under Apache-2.0 AND BSD-3-Clause. Its source, and the
full text of both licenses, are at the address above and in the crate
published at https://crates.io/crates/virtiofsd.

The binary is statically linked with:

  musl libc     MIT              https://musl.libc.org
  libseccomp    LGPL-2.1-only    https://github.com/seccomp/libseccomp
  libcap-ng     LGPL-2.1-or-later https://github.com/stevegrubb/libcap-ng

and with the Rust crates named in virtiofsd's Cargo.lock, under their own
licenses (mostly MIT or Apache-2.0).

To rebuild it yourself, with your own copies of the libraries above, run the
script HiveWorker builds it with, which needs only Docker:

  https://github.com/thecomputerplumbers/hiveworker/blob/main/daemon/release/virtiofsd.sh

You may use your distribution's virtiofsd instead: put it on your PATH and
remove HiveWorker's copy from its configuration directory.
